Exhibitions and World's Fairs

A research aid compiled from about fifty-five scholarly books and articles on the history of exhibitions.

Timeline and map 804 national and international exhibitions up to 1990 mentioned in the texts, including fairs that were planned but never held. A further 306 exhibitions of 1850–1950 that the texts do not mention can be switched on. Study pages 218 pages: the most-discussed exhibitions, 1800–1970, every exhibition in East and Southeast Asia our texts mention, and 88 imperial and colonial exhibitions: long description, annotated bibliography, Asian presence, primary sources online. Contents of the texts The table of contents of every book and article, with an overview of each chapter, a short overview of each work, and a note on how far it discusses East and Southeast Asia. Approaches The theoretical framings, concepts and methods used in the texts, in thirteen families, with 452 concepts defined and referenced and suggestions for carrying each approach over to a new case study. Bibliography 9,431 works cited across the texts, merged and deduplicated, with citation counts and Zotero import.
  • Data: timeline.csv, timeline_mentions.csv, timeline_not_in_texts.csv, bibliography.csv
  • Entries were extracted by machine reading and have not been checked one by one. Facts come only from the texts; a blank means no text gave the value.
  • The one exception is the separate list of exhibitions not in the texts, which comes from Wikipedia and other online sources and is hidden unless asked for.
